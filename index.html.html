🎯 Why it matters to you
💰

Lower bills

Less water used means a smaller water bill, less electricity for the pump, and fewer tanker trips.

🌾

Food for everyone

Farming uses about 80% of our fresh water. When cities waste water, villages and fields can run dry.

🧒

Healthy children

When clean water is short, people drink unsafe water. This causes illnesses like diarrhoea and typhoid.

👩‍👧

An easier life

In many villages, women and girls walk for kilometres to bring water. Saving water helps them too.

🌳

Nature stays alive

Rivers, ponds, trees and birds all depend on water. When we waste less, they survive.

🏠

A safe tomorrow

Groundwater takes many years to fill again. What we save today is what our children will drink.

EVERY DAY

Simple Ways to Save Water

Small habits. Big difference.

🪥

Brushing

Close the tap while brushing. Use a mug. Saves about 6 L a day.

🚿

Quick showers

Keep showers short or use a bucket. Saves up to 25 L each time.

🍽️

Full loads only

Run the washing machine and dishwasher only when full.

🔧

Fix drips

One dripping tap can waste over 1,500 L in a year.

🌱

Reuse water

Use rinse water from vegetables and rice for your plants.

🚽

Smart flushing

Use a dual-flush or put a filled bottle in the tank.

🚗

Bucket wash

Wash the car with a bucket, not a running hose.

🌧️

Catch rain

Collect rainwater for gardens and cleaning.
